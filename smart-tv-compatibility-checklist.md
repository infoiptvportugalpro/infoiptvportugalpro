# Smart TV Compatibility Checklist

Before choosing a streaming service or subscription, use this checklist to make sure your home setup is ready.

## 1. Check Your TV
- Identify your TV brand and model.
- Check which operating system your TV uses.
- Confirm that your TV supports the streaming applications you want to use.

## 2. Check Your Internet Connection
- Test your internet speed.
- Use a stable Wi-Fi connection or Ethernet where available.
- Reduce network congestion if videos buffer frequently.

## 3. Check Your Streaming Device
- Confirm compatibility with your TV's HDMI port.
- Keep the device firmware updated.
- Check available storage and app support.

## 4. Review Streaming App Compatibility
- Download applications only from trusted sources.
- Confirm that the service supports your device and region.
- Use authorized streaming services and subscriptions.

## 5. Improve Playback Quality
- Choose video quality suited to your internet connection.
- Restart your router or device if playback problems continue.
- Keep your TV and streaming apps updated.
